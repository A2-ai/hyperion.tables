## 1. Architecture summary

`hyperion.tables` is a pure R library (~10.4k LOC, S7 object system, no CLI/service
surface) that turns NONMEM model output — read via the private, trusted `hyperion`
upstream — into publication-ready parameter, model-comparison, and run-summary tables,
rendered to gt (HTML-first) and flextable (Word-first) with PNG and docx export. The
layering is clean and real: spec definition (`spec-*.R`) → data pipeline
(`parameter-apply.R` | `compare.R` | `summary.R`) → a format-agnostic `HyperionTable`
IR (`hyperion-table.R`) → renderers (`render-common.R` → `render-gt.R` |
`render-flextable.R`) → export. State crosses pipeline stages as data-frame attributes
(`hyperion_spec`, `hyperion_meta`, `model_summary`, …) — a convention, not a mechanism.

**Flows traced** (per README/vignettes): (a) parameter table:
`get_model_parameter_info() |> apply_table_spec(spec, info) |> make_parameter_table() |> render_to_*()`;
(b) comparison: two enriched frames through `compare_with()` (full join by `name`,
per-model `_1`/`_2`… suffixing, metadata bundle in `hyperion_meta`) →
`make_comparison_table()`; (c) summary: lineage tree → `apply_summary_spec()` (loads
models, computes dOFV/LRT) → `make_summary_table()`.

**Structural health.** The bones are good: one formatting choke point
(`apply_formatting()`, `render-common.R:13`) that both renderers share — so numeric
values, CI merging, and missing-text policy *cannot* drift between gt and flextable —
plus loud S7 guards at every entry point and an unusually good statistical-suppression
pattern (`rlang::inform` with reasons). The systemic weakness is **re-derivation instead
of single sources of truth** above that layer: effective-column resolution exists in ≥8
places with three incompatible precedence rules; LRT/dOFV exists twice with different
df definitions and gating; footnote statistic detection re-infers visibility from raw
data shapes; renderer styling (bold, borders, labels, footnotes) is implemented twice
against the IR's promise of format-agnosticism; and the docx exporter is ~600 lines of
byte-level contract with unpinned gt/pandoc, with zero tests. Because outputs land in
client-facing/regulatory reports, most consequences below are of the worst class:
**silently wrong numbers, footnotes, or labels**, not crashes.

**Hypothesis outcomes** (addendum §3 — refutations reported here as required):
- Target 1 (LRT/dOFV duplication): **confirmed, Critical** (Finding 1). Refined: in the
  default unfiltered configuration the two paths agree numerically (verified against
  paired summary/comparison snapshots; `lrt_pvalue` itself is shared and consistent) —
  the agreement is coincidental, not mechanical, and breaks under filters, column
  selections, and reversed comparison order.
- Target 2 (column-visibility triplication): **confirmed and worse** — eight sites,
  three precedence dialects, with drift already frozen into the snapshot corpus
  (Findings 3, 4).
- Target 3 (attribute-carried state): **confirmed but narrower than feared.** The spec
  attribute is an accidental canary: every `make_*` aborts loudly when it is stripped,
  so most user dplyr interposition fails safe. The genuinely silent residue is the
  comparison-chain metadata rebuild and the time-unit fallback (Finding 10).
- Target 4 (renderer parity): **confirmed for styling/labels/footnotes, refuted for
  data formatting** (shared `apply_formatting()`; paired gt/ft snapshots pin each
  renderer). Findings 8, 9.
- Target 5 (docx surgery): **confirmed**, plus a live output defect found in the surgery
  itself (Finding 5).
- Open hunt: name-based identity produced two more silent-wrong-number defects
  (Findings 6, 7); the survey's worst-case fear that positional name-matching could
  misalign names and estimates in `resolve_name_columns`/`apply_name_source` was
  **refuted** — matching is by key, and the all-or-nothing fallback fails visibly.
  Category C (concurrency) confirmed N/A.

## 2. Primary findings (ranked)

### Finding 1 — Comparison-table LRT is derived from the display-filtered join, so filters, column selection, and comparison order silently change the printed p-value

**Severity:** Critical · **Category:** D (+B) · **Confidence:** High (mechanism verified
line-by-line and cross-checked against snapshots; not executable here since `hyperion` is
absent; the reversed-order sub-case is Medium pending `are_models_in_lineage` semantics)

**Evidence:**
- df counted from the joined *display* frame: `R/compare.R:117-127` (`can_show_lrt`:
  `k1/k2 = sum(!is.na(fixed_N) & !fixed_N)` over comparison rows; `df <- abs(k2 - k1)`),
  duplicated at `R/compare-table.R:237-245`.
- Rows are filtered *before* the join: `apply_sections_and_filters`
  (`R/parameter-apply.R:231-249` — section `keep`/`exclude` + `row_filter`) runs inside
  `apply_table_spec`, whose output is what `compare_with` joins (`R/compare.R:481,528`).
- Correct-df reference implementation in the summary path: `R/summary.R:549-557`
  (df from `sum(!mod_sum$parameters$fixed)` over the **full** model parameter table),
  direction-enforced at `R/summary.R:685-690`.
- Sign convention: `delta_ofv <- ofv2 - ofv1`; `lrt_pvalue(-delta_ofv, df)`
  (`R/compare-table.R:236,254`) assumes the left model is the parent; nothing enforces
  that (left is the pct_change reference or simply chain order, `R/compare-table.R:341-356`,
  `R/compare.R:57-84`). `lrt_pvalue` is one-sided (`R/utils.R:97-112`).
- `fixed_*` columns exist in the join only when the spec's columns include CI or
  variability (`R/compare.R:159-161, 311-314`); the suppression `rlang::inform` at
  `R/compare-table.R:274-282` is unreachable when `df == 0` or `fixed_*` are absent
  (guards at `:240,:245` sit before it).

**The issue.** Degrees of freedom are a property of the two *models*, but the comparison
path computes them from whatever rows survived display filtering of a joined table. Three
concrete wrong-output modes:
1. A spec with `filter_rules(kind == "THETA")` (or a section filter) on a parent/child
   pair differing by 1 THETA + 1 OMEGA prints `df=1` and `pchisq(Δ, 1)` instead of df=2 —
   a wrong p-value, no warning. The summary table for the same pair prints df=2, so the
   two deliverables contradict each other.
2. If the filter hides *all* differing parameters, df=0 and the LRT vanishes silently
   (the explanatory inform is unreachable).
3. `compare_with(newer, older)` (reversed order): using fixture ground truth
   (run003 vs run002: Δ=6.358, df=1 → p=0.0117 per the summary snapshot), the reversed
   comparison computes `-delta_ofv = −6.358` → prints "LRT p-value = 1 (df=1)". Because
   `df <- abs(k2 − k1)` is deliberately direction-agnostic while the statistic's sign is
   not, this is a wrong number if `are_models_in_lineage()` is symmetric (question for
   maintainers) — and a cross-path inconsistency even if it isn't.
Additionally, LRT availability is coupled to the user's *column* selection: a spec of
`columns = c("name","estimate","rse")` produces no `fixed_*` columns and suppresses the
footnote silently, while the summary table still shows the p-value.

**Blast radius:** every comparison table built with row/section filters, restricted
columns, or non-lineage ordering — silent, in the package's highest-stakes output
(statistical footnotes in regulatory-style reports; maintainer priority [Q8]).

**Remediation sketch:** compute LRT inputs from model summaries, not displayed rows.
Capture `k = sum(!parameters$fixed)` per model at `apply_table_spec` time (same
definition as `R/summary.R:554`) and carry it in `hyperion_meta`; make `can_show_lrt`
the single gate returning `list(show, reason, df, delta)` (deleting the duplicated
gating in `format_ofv_lrt_footnote`); orient the test by lineage (stat =
OFV(reduced) − OFV(full), df = k_full − k_reduced, suppress when the pair isn't
ancestor-ordered). Longer term, one internal `lrt_for_pair(parent_sum, child_sum)`
serving both table types kills the split-brain (see also Finding 8). Effort: ~1-2 days
+ maintainer-side snapshot regeneration.

### Finding 2 — Default numeric formatter switches to scientific notation for any value that rounds to ≥ 10^n_sigfig; `1000` renders as `"1e+03"` in body cells

**Severity:** High (borderline Critical) · **Category:** B/F · **Confidence:** High
(empirically verified by executing the extracted function)

**Evidence:** `format_sigfig_pad` returns `formatC(x, digits = n_sigfig, format = "g")`
output verbatim whenever `%g` chooses scientific notation (`R/variability.R:43-46`). It
is not confined to the variability column: `format_numeric_for_table`
(`R/render-common.R:48-60`) unions **all** numeric columns for parameter and comparison
tables and formats them with `formatter = NULL`, which routes to this function
(`R/render-common.R:258-266`), as do `format_value` (`:234-236`) and the CI-merge path
(`:194-199`).

**The issue.** Verified outputs at the default `n_sigfig = 3`: `1000 → "1e+03"`,
`999.6 → "1e+03"`, `1200 → "1.2e+03"`, `1500 → "1.5e+03"`, `-1234.5 → "-1.23e+03"`,
`99999 → "1e+05"`; also `1e-05 → "1e-05"` (one significant digit shown, inconsistent
with the 3-sig-fig padding elsewhere). Estimates, SEs, CI bounds, CVs, and SDs ≥ ~999.5
— volumes of distribution, AUCs, additive-error SDs in concentration units are routinely
in this range — render in scientific notation mid-table. The snapshot corpus contains no
value crossing the threshold (no `e+0` occurs in `_snaps/`), so no test pins either
behavior. Below the threshold the padding/rounding arithmetic was verified **correct**
against a `signif`-based reference on 40+ adversarial inputs (negatives, zero,
9.996→"10.0", 0.09996→"0.100", trailing-zero significance).

**Blast radius:** any table containing a large-magnitude estimate — silent, cosmetic-
looking-but-unacceptable in client-facing deliverables.

**Remediation sketch:** in `format_sigfig_pad`, convert the `%g`-scientific branch to
fixed notation (`formatC(format = "fg")` or `format(signif(x, n), scientific = FALSE)`)
and keep the existing padding; adopt an explicit policy for extreme magnitudes (e.g.
switch to scientific only beyond ~1e6/1e-4, uniformly padded). Hours.

### Finding 3 — Footnote statistic detection is blind to suffixed and render-hidden columns: comparison tables both under- and over-disclose statistics (already frozen in snapshots)

**Severity:** High · **Category:** A (systemic duplication) with B-class consequence ·
**Confidence:** High (snapshot-proven)

**Evidence:**
- `detect_table_statistics` (`R/format.R:186-243`) tests **unsuffixed** columns for
  cv/sd/corr (`:203-205` via `"cv" %in% names(params)`), stderr/shrinkage (`:234-237`),
  and hard-codes `rse_1`/`rse_2` only (`:226-228`); only CI scans all suffixes (`:220`).
  CV-formula detection additionally needs an unsuffixed `transforms` column (`:209-217`).
- Comparison frames carry only suffixed stats (`R/compare.R:304-325`) and drop
  `transforms` at join time (`R/compare.R:439-441`); comparison footnotes call the
  detector on that frame (`R/compare-table.R:690`).
- Substrate asymmetry: parameter tables physically remove dropped columns
  (`order_sections`, `R/parameter-table.R:166-226`) so data-presence ≈ visibility;
  comparison tables only hide at render (`compute_comparison_layout`,
  `R/compare-table.R:869-921`) while footnotes are built earlier (`:623`).
- **Ground truth:** `tests/testthat/_snaps/comparison-table/cmp-no-offdiag-var-gt-snapshot.html`
  (default columns, `drop_columns = c("ci","rse","shrinkage")`): body cells show
  `(CV = 37.4%)` etc., yet there is **no** "CV = coefficient of variation" and no CV%
  formula equations — while the abbreviations footnote **does** say "RSE = relative
  standard error" with zero RSE columns in the table (verified: 0 matches for `rse`).
  Every parameter-table snapshot with CV values carries the CV formulas; zero comparison
  snapshots do.
- Related corner: dropping one model's CI (`ci_1`/`ci_left`) expands to a *global*
  `ci_low`+`ci_high` drop in `expand_ci_drop_columns` (`R/spec-validation.R:99-102`) →
  `has_ci = FALSE` while the other model's CI column remains visible.

**The issue.** "Which statistics does this table show?" is answered by re-inferring from
raw data shapes and a private re-derivation of spec columns, instead of from the final
visible layout. For comparisons the answer is systematically wrong in both directions:
statistics displayed without disclosure (missing CV/SD/Corr abbreviations and CV%
formulas — the exact footnote class the maintainer ranked highest), and disclosures for
statistics not displayed (RSE). The snapshot suite blesses the wrong output, so no
regression will ever flag it.

**Blast radius:** every comparison table with variability/CV content — i.e. the default
spec shape — plus ≥3-model comparisons (rse_3+ never detected) and per-model CI drops.

**Remediation sketch:** after layout is computed, derive the canonical visible-column
set (`setdiff(names(data), hide_cols)`, strip `_\d+$` suffixes) and pass it to
`detect_table_statistics`; make all stat checks suffix-aware (`^cv(_\d+)?$` etc., the
existing `has_rse_comparison` idiom generalized); carry `transforms` through the join
(add to coalesce columns) for CV-formula detection. ~1-2 days + snapshot regeneration
(maintainer-side per Q9).

### Finding 4 — Effective-column resolution has no single source of truth: ≥8 sites, three incompatible precedence rules, and an exported getter that contradicts its own documentation

**Severity:** High · **Category:** A · **Confidence:** High

**Evidence (the re-derivations):**
- `get_spec_columns` (`R/spec-getters.R:24-33`) returns `(columns %||% default) ∪ add`
  and **never subtracts `drop_columns`**, while its roxygen (`:12-14`) and
  `drop_spec_columns()`'s docs promise the subtraction.
- `order_sections` (`R/parameter-table.R:166-172`): `setdiff(base, expand_ci(drop)) ∪ add`
  → **add beats drop**.
- `resolve_hidden_columns` (`R/parameter-table.R:440-478`): `requested = columns ∪ add`;
  **drop ignored entirely**.
- `detect_table_statistics` (`R/format.R:190-200`): `setdiff(base ∪ add, drop)` →
  **drop beats add**.
- `wants_variability_column` honors drop (`R/variability.R:140-144`);
  `wants_variability_components` ignores it (`:147-153`).
- `get_comparison_suffix_cols` (`R/compare.R:147-163`): `(columns %||% default) − name`;
  **add and drop both ignored** — so `add_columns` silently no-ops for comparison
  tables (only `pct_change` consults it, `R/compare.R:316-326`), and the join discards
  non-suffixed columns (`:439-441`).
- Hand-patches acknowledging the gap: `maybe_enrich_description`
  (`R/parameter-apply.R:98-101`) re-implements drop subtraction inline (its test at
  `test-parameter-table.R:909-933` pins drop-beats-add as intended semantics);
  `R/render-common.R:104-107` does a raw `"df" %in% spec@drop_columns` check.

**The issue.** Hand-traced disagreements, both silent:
- `TableSpec(columns = c("name","estimate","cv"), drop_columns = "cv")`: the cv column
  is **displayed** (`resolve_hidden_columns` never consults drop) while
  `detect_table_statistics` excludes it → visible statistic with no footnote — Finding
  3's defect class reproduced in single-model parameter tables.
- `TableSpec() |> add_spec_columns("cv") |> drop_spec_columns("cv")`: documented
  semantics say back-to-default; actual result hides variability **and** shows cv — the
  opposite table.
- `TableSpec(add_columns = "stderr")`: parameter table gains an SE column; the same spec
  through `make_comparison_table` shows nothing, no warning. Worse, `add_columns = "cv"`
  flips `wants_variability_components` TRUE → variability is not built and cv is never
  suffixed: the add strictly *degrades* the comparison.
This class has already fired in the wild: NEWS 0.5.0's comparison-drops-columns bug fix
and the comment at `R/parameter-table.R:483` ("single check, was previously duplicated").
Every future column feature must pick one of three semantics at random.

**Blast radius:** change amplification on every column-related feature across all three
table types; user-visible contract violations on an exported getter.

**Remediation sketch:** implement the documented rule once — internal
`resolve_effective_columns(spec)` = `(columns %||% default) ∪ add − expand_ci_drop(drop)`
with drop-beats-add (confirm intent, see Questions) — and convert the eight sites into
consumers; comparison suffix selection = resolver minus `name`; either support
`add_columns` in comparisons via the resolver or warn explicitly. ~2-3 days + snapshot
churn. Findings 3 and 4 together define the single pre-render "layout + disclosure"
boundary the codebase is missing.

### Finding 5 — The docx exporter is an untested byte-level contract with unpinned gt/pandoc; it currently emits literal `**` around math-bearing headers, and its failure modes are silent no-op or destroyed output

**Severity:** High · **Category:** B/E · **Confidence:** High for the live defect's
mechanism (static trace, every link verified; deps absent so not executed) and for the
systemic claims

**Evidence:**
- Live defect — helper ordering: `sanitize_gt_docx` runs `bold_header_rows`
  (`R/render-gt.R:446`) before `rewrite_latex_to_omml` (`:450`).
  `run_text_with_markdown` (`:928-947`) reconstructs any bold run as `**…**`;
  `bold_header_rows` (`:648-668`) synthesizes `<w:b/>` on *every* header run; the
  default summary/comparison label map contains `dofv = "$\Delta$OFV"` (`R/format.R:147`,
  wired via `R/summary.R:960` and md-wrapped at `R/render-gt.R:150-152`). The math
  splitter then emits the `**` as literal text parts (`:982`). Net: every
  `render_to_word()` of a summary or comparison table shows `**ΔOFV**` in the Word
  header (as does any user-supplied `$…$` label).
- Destructive rewrite: `gtsave` writes the final path (`:410`); after sanitizing,
  `unlink(path); zip_dir_contents(stage, path)` (`:459-460`). A zip failure (disk full,
  file locked by Word/OneDrive) leaves the user with **no file**; a failure *before* the
  unlink leaves a silently **unsanitized** docx exhibiting the repair-prompt defects the
  pipeline exists to fix.
- Unpinned byte contract: each of ~12 helpers assumes specific gt/pandoc emission
  details — lowercase `caption` style id (`:552-560`), `w:tblHeader` markers (`:591`),
  missing `w:tblGrid` (`:739-779`), hard-coded 18- and 33-element XSD orderings
  (`:808-869`), `SEQ Table` field shape (`:675-709`), `$…$` surviving as literal text
  (`:872-947`). Every helper is a **silent no-op** when its XPath stops matching, so a
  gt or pandoc upgrade re-introduces Word repair prompts with no error. DESCRIPTION pins
  only `gt >= 1.0.0`; pandoc (named in the code's own comments as the emitter being
  patched, `:421-424,873`) is not versionable in DESCRIPTION at all. Zero tests: none of
  the 19 function names in this path (including `render_to_word`) appears anywhere under
  `tests/` (verified by grep).
- `dedupe_xmlns_w` (`:954-965`) does namespace dedup via global `gsub` on the raw
  serialized bytes — a cell or footnote whose *text* contains the literal
  ` xmlns:w="…main"` string would be silently mutated; duplicate same-URI declarations
  are schema-valid, so the helper's premise deserves confirmation.
- `rewrite_latex_to_omml` splices `equatags::transform_mathjax(to = "mml")` output —
  MathML — where the wrapper and comments promise OMML (`:1028-1043` vs `:904-912`);
  no MML→OMML conversion exists (flextable's path has one). Works-by-accident risk;
  listed under Questions.

**Blast radius:** every Word deliverable (the regulatory-facing format) — a live
cosmetic defect today, plus data-loss and silent-degradation modes armed by any
environment refresh (new RStudio bundles a new pandoc; `rv` upgrade bumps gt).

**Remediation sketch:** (a) reorder `rewrite_latex_to_omml` before `bold_header_rows`,
or mark synthesized `<w:b>` nodes and skip them in the markdown reconstruction — hours,
fixes the live defect; (b) zip to a tempfile and rename over `path`; wrap sanitization
so failure restores or clearly labels the artifact — hours; (c) add the two-tier test
harness (pure-helper units on string fixtures + a checked-in raw `gtsave` docx fixture
with post-sanitize invariants: parses, one `w:tblGrid` per table, no field codes, unique
xmlns, no literal `**` in header text, compat mode 15) with a helper-matched-zero-nodes
warning so upstream fixes are noticed and helpers retired — 1-2 days, maintainer-side
given Q9.

### Finding 6 — `compare_with()` joins on `name` alone: cross-kind name collisions silently fan out rows and pair wrong estimates

**Severity:** High · **Category:** D · **Confidence:** Medium-high (mechanism verified;
trigger frequency depends on comment-naming habits)

**Evidence:** `dplyr::full_join(..., by = "name")` at `R/compare.R:481` and `:528`;
`kind` is a coalesce column, not a join key (`R/compare.R:344,489-504,530-540`); no
duplicate-name guard exists upstream (`apply_name_source`, `R/parameter-apply.R:531-544`,
matches by name+kind — but the join doesn't).

**The issue.** With `parameter_names` sourced from comments, an OMEGA commented "CL"
(IIV on CL) collides with a THETA named "CL". If model A has one "CL" row and model B
has two, the one-to-many join silently duplicates A's row; one output row pairs A's
THETA estimate with B's OMEGA variance; the coalesced `kind` shows A's "THETA", masking
the mismatch; `pct_change` is computed across kinds. dplyr warns only on many-to-many,
not one-to-many.

**Blast radius:** comparison tables with comment-derived names — duplicated and
cross-matched parameter rows in client-facing tables, no error.

**Remediation sketch:** join by `c("name", "kind")` (both frames already carry `kind`)
and abort/warn on duplicated (name, kind) pairs before joining. Hours.

### Finding 7 — Summary pipeline collapses model identity to basename stems: colliding stems silently load the wrong model and compute dOFV against the wrong parent

**Severity:** High (conditional on multi-directory lineage trees) · **Category:** D ·
**Confidence:** High on mechanism

**Evidence:** `build_metadata_df` keys everything on
`tools::file_path_sans_ext(basename(node$name))` (`R/summary.R:200`, `based_on`
likewise at `:204`); `load_models` stores `models[name] <- list(mod)` (`:279`) — a
duplicate stem **overwrites** the first model; `calculate_dofv` does
`setNames(df$ofv, df$.name)` lookups where `[[` on duplicated names silently returns
the first; `filter_metadata` matches `models_to_include` by lower-cased stem
(`:154-157`).

**The issue.** A lineage tree containing `base/run001.mod` and `covariate/run001.mod`
yields two table rows backed by one loaded model; any child based on either gets dOFV
against whichever "run001" won the overwrite. No warning fires anywhere.

**Blast radius:** silently wrong OFV/dOFV/p-values in run-summary tables for any
project organized as parallel model directories.

**Remediation sketch:** key `.name`, the `models` list, and all lookups by the full
`node$name` path; stem only the display `model` column; add an `anyDuplicated()`
assertion in `build_metadata_df`. Hours.

### Finding 8 — dOFV/LRT never checks estimation-method parity, and the two implementations differ on four axes — default-case agreement is coincidental

**Severity:** Medium (High if mixed-method trees are common) · **Category:** D/A ·
**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** `calculate_dofv` gates only on parent presence, nobs equality, non-NA OFV
(`R/summary.R:647-691`); `can_show_lrt` gates on ofv/nobs/df/lineage
(`R/compare.R:91-144`); neither compares `estimation_method`, though it is already
fetched from `run_details`. OFV is taken from the **last** `minimization_results` row
(`R/summary.R:571-580`), i.e. the final `$EST`. The two implementations further differ
on df source (Finding 1), direction handling (`abs` vs strictly child>parent,
`R/summary.R:687`), pair selection (pct_change reference/chain order vs `based_on[1]`),
and lineage verification (filesystem check vs none). All fixtures are single-`$EST`
FOCE, so nothing exercises any of this.

**The issue.** Parent run FOCE-I, child ending `METHOD=IMP EONLY=1` (or SAEM): the OFVs
are on different likelihood approximations, and the printed dOFV/LRT p-value is
statistically meaningless — exactly the failure class the existing nobs gate was built
for. Meanwhile the four-axis divergence means the comparison and summary tables can test
*different pairs* with *different df* under non-default configuration.

**Blast radius:** multi-method model trees; silent, statistically wrong footnotes.

**Remediation sketch:** add an estimation-method equality gate with an `rlang::inform`
mirroring the nobs message (hours); fold both paths into the single `lrt_for_pair()`
from Finding 1's remediation (1-2 days, shared effort).

### Finding 9 — The HyperionTable IR is not actually renderer-agnostic: bold/borders/labels/footnotes are per-renderer conventions, with one live divergence and a byte-coupled fallback that can emit a wrong formula

**Severity:** Medium · **Category:** A/D · **Confidence:** High (fallback drift
mechanically demonstrated in scratchpad)

**Evidence:**
- Live divergence: gt honors all four `bold_locations` values (`R/render-gt.R:185-216`);
  flextable honors only `"column_labels"` and hard-bolds title/spanners/groups
  (`R/render-flextable.R:313-324,338,301,109`). Comparison tables set
  `bold_locs <- c("column_labels","spanners")` (`R/compare-table.R:637-640`) → titled
  comparisons render **non-bold in gt, bold in flextable**.
- The black model-boundary border exists only in the gt path
  (`R/render-gt.R:66-83,95-116` + a custom attr channel into docx surgery, `:409`);
  flextable comparisons never get it, and apply their gray borders `part = "all"`
  (header included) where gt applies body-only (`R/render-flextable.R:365-368` vs
  `R/render-gt.R:227`).
- Byte-coupled footnote fallback: when equatags is absent, flextable transliterates the
  LaTeX footnotes via three hard-coded whole-formula regexes
  (`R/render-flextable.R:502-524`) that must byte-match strings assembled in
  `build_equations_footnote` (`R/format.R:274-366`), plus a generic `[^}]+`
  transliterator that cannot handle nested braces (`:527-544`). Today all five match
  (verified by transliterating both sides in a scratchpad); after an innocuous edit like
  `\times 100` → `\cdot 100`, the fallback emits `sqrt(exp(Estimate)) - 1 · 100` — the
  `- 1` migrates outside the sqrt: a plausible-looking, mathematically wrong CV formula.
  All committed flextable snapshots were generated *with* equatags (they contain katex
  markup), so the fallback has zero coverage.
- Label dialects: gt md-wraps labels containing `$` (`R/render-gt.R:149-153`);
  flextable's `convert_md_label` knows only Δ/δ then strips `$`
  (`R/render-flextable.R:249-257`) — any new LaTeX label renders correctly in gt and as
  raw `\theta` in flextable. gt additionally runs `fmt_markdown()` on **all** body cells
  (`:38-39`) while flextable renders body text verbatim: `ModelComments`-sourced names
  containing `*`, `_`, `[` render differently per renderer.

**Blast radius:** same spec → visibly different regulatory deliverables depending on
`output=`; every styling feature must be implemented twice with no signal when it isn't;
the no-equatags configuration (equatags is Suggests, warned once at load) silently
degrades statistical footnotes after any format.R edit.

**Remediation sketch:** make flextable read `bold_locations`; move the boundary border
into `@borders` so both renderers and the docx pass consume one spec; replace the
regex fallback by structuring `build_equations_footnote()`'s return
(`list(parts = list(text=…, latex=…))`) so gt markdown, flextable equations, and a
plain-text form derive from one value — or hard-require equatags for flextable and
delete the fallback (maintainer call, see Questions). ~1-2 days total.

### Finding 10 — The two silent attribute-loss paths: chained comparisons fabricate empty metadata, and the time-unit fallback guesses wrong at every decidable branch

**Severity:** Medium · **Category:** D/B · **Confidence:** High (attr-stripping behavior
verified empirically in R 4.5.3: `subset()`/`df[i,j]` keep the `hyperion_comparison`
class but drop `hyperion_meta`)

**Evidence:**
- `compare_with(m1, m2, labels=…) |> subset(…) |> compare_with(m3) |> make_comparison_table()`:
  the class survives so the guard at `R/compare-table.R:443` passes, but
  `compute_model_positions` rebuilds meta from nothing — `NULL`-padded summaries and
  synthesized "Model N" labels (`R/compare.R:366-377`), user labels lost, OFV/dOFV/LRT
  footnotes for the earlier pair silently absent (`format_ofv_lrt_footnote` returns
  NULL with no message when OFVs are NA, `R/compare-table.R:210-212`), and lost
  `pct_change_refs` makes `resolve_reference_context` (`R/compare.R:57-84`) fall back to
  positional reference — the footnote can **misattribute which model a %-change is
  relative to**. Zero conditions raised.
- `get_time_suffix` (`R/summary.R:853-894`): when the `summary_time_unit` attr is lost
  under `time_format = "auto"`, it guesses units from *already-divided* values — but
  post-division data with max < 1 can only be seconds (fallback says "h") and max ≥ 60
  can only be hours (fallback says "s"); the middle returns "min" (wrong whenever the
  true unit was hours: an 8-hour run labels as "8 (min)"). Every decidable branch is
  inverted. Reachable because the manual attr round-trip at `R/summary.R:113-118`
  protects only `select_output_columns` while the `summary_filter` `dplyr::filter` runs
  after it (`:121-126`) — and DESCRIPTION declares **unversioned** `dplyr`, though the
  package's attr survival requires ≥ 1.0 `dplyr_reconstruct` semantics. Two
  contradictory doctrines coexist: the comparison path capture/restores with comments
  asserting dplyr strips attrs (`R/compare.R:7-8`), while parameter/summary paths pipe
  attrs through dplyr bare (`R/parameter-table.R:394-401`, `R/summary.R:111-131`).
- Misleading diagnostic: when meta *is* fully stripped, `make_comparison_table` aborts
  with "Run `apply_table_spec()` first" (`R/compare-table.R:450-455`) — the wrong remedy;
  the user did run it, and the cause is `subset()`/`[` stripping.

**Blast radius:** chained comparisons and summary tables touched by user dplyr between
stages — the documented data-frame-based API invites exactly that.

**Remediation sketch:** treat "class present, meta absent" as the attr-stripping
signature and abort/warn there with a cause-naming message; delete the time-unit
heuristic (warn + honest fallback, or carry the unit in the spec/label map); declare
`dplyr (>= 1.0.0)` and pick one attr doctrine. Hours, plus one vignette sentence
("subsetting the data frame drops table metadata; filter via the spec").

## 4. Questions for maintainers

1. **Is `hyperion::are_models_in_lineage(a, b)` symmetric?** Decides whether Finding 1's
   reversed-order case prints p≈1 (wrong number) or is suppressed (cross-path
   divergence only).
2. **Can a lineage tree legitimately contain models in different directories sharing a
   basename stem?** Decides Finding 7's practical severity.
3. **Intended column precedence:** drop-beats-add (as `maybe_enrich_description`, its
   test, and the setter docs imply)? The Finding 4 resolver's semantics depend on it.
   Relatedly: is `add_columns` meant to work for comparison tables at all?
4. Should footnote abbreviations describe only *visible* statistics (Finding 3's RSE
   case suggests yes), or is "used in derivation, even if hidden" ever desired?
5. Is a comparison's LRT pair *intended* to follow the pct_change `reference_model`
   rather than the lineage parent when one is set (`R/compare-table.R:346-353`)? The
   summary table always pairs against `based_on[1]`, so the two tables can test
   different pairs by design.
6. Is cross-estimation-method dOFV deliberately left to user judgment (Finding 8), or
   should it be gated like nobs?
7. Is "flextable without equatags" a supported configuration? If not, hard-require it
   and the fragile footnote fallback (Finding 9) can be deleted outright.
8. Does Word genuinely accept the raw MathML that `equatags::transform_mathjax(to="mml")`
   returns spliced into `document.xml` (Finding 5)? flextable does an explicit MML→OMML
   XSLT; this path doesn't. And what Word behavior motivated `dedupe_xmlns_w`? Duplicate
   same-URI declarations are schema-valid — if Word tolerates them, the riskiest helper
   is deletable.
9. Was `get_time_suffix`'s value fallback written for *undivided* raw-seconds data? As
   reachable today the data is always pre-divided, which is what inverts its branches.
10. `summary_filter` runs after `select_output_columns` (`R/summary.R:122-127`) —
    intentional that filters can only reference displayed columns?

## 5. What's good (preserve while fixing the rest)

- **`apply_formatting()` as the single formatting choke point** (`R/render-common.R:13-45`)
  behind the `HyperionTable` IR: numeric values, CI merging, and missing-text policy are
  mechanically identical across gt, flextable, and the `output = "data"` escape hatch.
  This is the pattern Findings 3, 4, and 9 should be fixed *into* — one pre-render
  layout/disclosure layer, both renderers inherit it.
- **Explained statistical suppression.** The `can_show_lrt` reason-string design
  (`R/compare.R:91-144`) and the `rlang::inform` explanations for dOFV/nobs
  (`R/summary.R:660-670`) are exactly right for regulatory work — extend this pattern
  (Findings 1, 8, and the silent-NA dOFV case) rather than replacing it.
- **Loud boundaries.** S7 validators plus `S7_inherits` guards on every exported
  function, spec-attribute checks that abort clearly at each `make_*` entry, and
  error translation like `with_section_options_error` (`R/spec-setters.R:443-465`) that
  speaks in user-argument language. The spec attr acting as a stripping canary is
  accidental but load-bearing — keep it while making the comparison-meta case (Finding
  10) equally loud.
- **Heavy-dependency hygiene:** renderer/export deps in Suggests with consistent
  `check_suggested()` gating (`R/utils.R:23`) keeps the core pipeline light.
